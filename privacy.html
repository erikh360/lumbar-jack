Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Lumbar Jack is a macOS menu bar application developed as an independent project.

Data Collection

Lumbar Jack collects no data of any kind. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, analytics, or telemetry.

Network Access

Lumbar Jack makes no network requests. It has no internet connectivity and cannot communicate with external servers.

Local Storage

All settings (reminder mode, durations, exercise preferences) are stored locally on your Mac using macOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

Third Parties

Lumbar Jack contains no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising frameworks, or tracking libraries of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Lumbar Jack does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated policy will be posted at this URL prior to the update shipping.
